## 1. Brand Positioning

### Recommended public positioning

Use **Aleppo CPC** in English as the umbrella brand for the club/community.

Use **مجتمع البرمجة التنافسية في جامعة حلب** or **نادي البرمجة التنافسية في جامعة حلب** in Arabic for the club/community.

Use **المسابقة البرمجية الجامعية في جامعة حلب** only when referring to the university-level event itself.

Use **ACPC** only for the Arab/Africa regional championship context to avoid name confusion.

### Core brand statement

English:
**Aleppo CPC is the official competitive programming community around ICPC at the University of Aleppo, building problem-solvers from campus contests to regional and world finals.**

Arabic:
**Aleppo CPC هو المجتمع الرسمي للبرمجة التنافسية المرتبط بمسار ICPC في جامعة حلب، ويطوّر المواهب من مسابقات الجامعة إلى المنافسات الإقليمية والنهائيات العالمية.**

### Mission

English:
Develop algorithmic thinking, teamwork under pressure, and a strong bridge between students, coaches, the university, and industry.

Arabic:
تنمية التفكير الخوارزمي والعمل الجماعي تحت الضغط، وبناء جسر فعّال بين الطلبة والمدربين والجامعة والقطاع التقني.

### Vision

English:
To make the University of Aleppo a leading ICPC pipeline in Syria and the Arab/Africa region.

Arabic:
أن تكون جامعة حلب مساراً رائداً نحو ICPC في سورية وعلى مستوى العرب وأفريقيا.

## 3. Audience Priorities

### Students
- Want a clear entry point, training path, event dates, eligibility, and proof that the journey can lead to major results.

### Coaches
- Need the ICPC structure, team rules, training calendar, qualification logic, and a place that reflects seriousness and continuity.

### Sponsors
- Need credibility, scale, media visibility, social impact, talent access, and a direct explanation of what sponsorship delivers.

### Visitors and university stakeholders
- Need a concise explanation of what ICPC is, why Aleppo CPC matters, and how the university has performed over time.

## 8. Recommended Proof Points to Feature

Use these as rotating proof blocks, timeline items, or sponsor-facing credibility markers.

- University of Aleppo has earned medals at the Arab/Africa level and reached ICPC world finals multiple times.
- Source material highlights world finals participation in 2016, 2019, 2021, 2023, and 2025 qualification to the Dubai finals.
- Source material highlights 2024 as champion and runner-up in Syria.
- Source material highlights 2025 as four medals out of ten in Syria, plus gold and bronze at the Arab/Africa level.
- The 2025 sponsorship request cites around 230 participants in total, including 210 contestants and 20 coaches or supervisors, plus roughly 100 volunteers.
- Event operations material shows that contests rely on large volunteer and support teams across media, logistics, operations, technical support, and judging.
- The sponsorship request frames the competition as a direct investment in student skills, teamwork, and readiness for the technical job market.

## 11. Tone and Copy Guidelines

- Use **competitive programming** instead of generic terms like “coding events” except in casual outreach copy.
- In Arabic, prefer **البرمجة التنافسية** for the discipline and **المسابقة البرمجية** for a specific contest.
- Keep sponsor copy credible and specific. Lead with talent, impact, and visibility rather than vague “support innovation” language.
- Keep student copy ambitious but practical. Focus on training, team experience, medals, and career readiness.
- Avoid overloading the homepage with operational detail; move procedures and documents into event pages and team pages.

## 14. Bottom-Line Recommendation

The website should present **Aleppo CPC** as three things at once:
- a serious student talent pipeline around ICPC
- a proven record of university and regional achievement
- a credible sponsorship platform with visible educational impact

If the site gets those three messages right in both Arabic and English, it will serve students, coaches, sponsors, and visitors without sounding fragmented.
