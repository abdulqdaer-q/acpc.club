## Accuracy guardrails — do not drift from these

- **135 registered teams**, **~500 registered contestants**, **~100 volunteers**.
- 500 does not divide into 135 teams of three (135 × 3 = 405). Describe it as
  registrations, never as the competing headcount.
- Growth is **~105%** against 66 teams in 2025. Both **98%** and **127%** appear in
  older drafts of club material — neither is current.
- Do **not** call the club an ICPC chapter, branch, or official representative.
  "Independent student club" is accurate and is what the site says.
- Competitive results belong to the contestants, coaches, organisers, alumni, and
  the university collectively — not to the club alone.
- The first World Finals season is labelled **2016** on the site but **2015** in the
  profile document. Unresolved; avoid naming that year in the post.

## If you ever want AI-generated backdrops

The generator uses the club's own photographs, which is the right default — real
students beat invented ones, and inventing team photos for a real launch
misrepresents the club. If you need an abstract backdrop for a future slide,
generate imagery **with no text in it** and let the generator lay type over it;
image models cannot render Arabic, which is the whole reason these slides are HTML.
